食物 shíwù · Food

水

shuǐ

water

← 牛奶稀飯 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    水在哪裡？我們來找水！
    Shuǐ zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo shuǐ!
    Where is the water? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then at the table or in the kitchen.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是水。
    Duì! Zhè shì shuǐ.
    Yes! This is the water.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    喝水，好舒服！
    Hē shuǐ, hǎo shūfú!
    Drink water... ahh, nice!
    🎭 Take a real sip of water together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 水在哪裡？
    Shuǐ zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the water? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 水冰冰的還是熱熱的？
    Shuǐ bīngbīng de háishì rèrè de?
    Is the water cold or warm?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
