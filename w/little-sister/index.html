家人 jiārén · Family

妹妹

mèimei

little sister

← 弟弟寶寶 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    妹妹在哪裡？我們來找妹妹！
    Mèimei zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo mèimei!
    Where is little sister? Let's go and look!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then to the real person if they are nearby (or a photo at home).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是妹妹。
    Duì! Zhè shì mèimei.
    Yes! This is little sister.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    妹妹笑嘻嘻！
    Mèimei xiàoxīxī!
    Little sister is giggling!
    🎭 Make a big smile and giggle.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 妹妹在哪裡？
    Mèimei zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is little sister? (point!)
  2. 這是誰？
    Zhè shì shéi?
    Who is this?
  3. 妹妹穿什麼顏色的衣服？
    Mèimei chuān shénme yánsè de yīfu?
    What color is little sister wearing?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
