自然 zìrán · Nature

雲

yún

cloud

← 天空雨 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    雲在哪裡？我們來找雲！
    Yún zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo yún!
    Where is the cloud? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look out of the window for the real one.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是雲。
    Duì! Zhè shì yún.
    Yes! This is the cloud.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    白雲飄啊飄！
    Báiyún piāo a piāo!
    The white cloud floats and floats!
    🎭 Float your arms slowly like a cloud.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 雲在哪裡？
    Yún zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the cloud? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 雲像什麼？
    Yún xiàng shénme?
    What does the cloud look like?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
