交通工具 jiāotōng gōngjù · Transportation

垃圾車

lèsèchē

garbage truck

← 救護車

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    垃圾車在哪裡？我們來找垃圾車！
    Lèsèchē zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo lèsèchē!
    Where is the garbage truck? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look for a real one outside.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是垃圾車。
    Duì! Zhè shì lèsèchē.
    Yes! This is the garbage truck.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    垃圾車來了，音樂響了！
    Lèsèchē lái le, yīnyuè xiǎng le!
    The garbage truck is coming, hear the music!
    🎭 Hum the garbage-truck tune and carry a pretend bag out.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 垃圾車在哪裡？
    Lèsèchē zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the garbage truck? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 聽到音樂，要做什麼？
    Tīng dào yīnyuè, yào zuò shénme?
    When we hear the music, what do we do? (take out the trash)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
