家裡的東西 jiālǐ de dōngxi · Things at Home

杯子

bēizi

cup

← 鏡子碗 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    杯子在哪裡？我們去找杯子！
    Bēizi zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo bēizi!
    Where is the cup? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是杯子。
    Duì! Zhè shì bēizi.
    Yes! This is the cup.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    乾杯！
    Gānbēi!
    Cheers!
    🎭 Clink cups gently.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 杯子在哪裡？
    Bēizi zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the cup? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 杯子裡面有什麼？
    Bēizi lǐmiàn yǒu shénme?
    What's in the cup?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
