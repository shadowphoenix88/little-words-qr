食物 shíwù · Food

餅乾

bǐnggān

cookie

← 稀飯起司 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    餅乾在哪裡？我們來找餅乾！
    Bǐnggān zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo bǐnggān!
    Where is the cookie? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then at the table or in the kitchen.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是餅乾。
    Duì! Zhè shì bǐnggān.
    Yes! This is the cookie.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    餅乾脆脆的，好好吃！
    Bǐnggān cuìcuì de, hǎo hǎochī!
    The cookie is crunchy and yummy!
    🎭 Pretend to bite: crunch crunch.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 餅乾在哪裡？
    Bǐnggān zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the cookie? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 你要幾片餅乾？
    Nǐ yào jǐ piàn bǐnggān?
    How many cookies do you want?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
