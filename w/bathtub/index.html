浴缸

yùgāng

bathtub

← 馬桶樓梯 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    浴缸在哪裡？我們去找浴缸！
    Yùgāng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo yùgāng!
    Where is the bathtub? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是浴缸。
    Duì! Zhè shì yùgāng.
    Yes! This is the bathtub.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來洗澡！
    Wǒmen lái xǐzǎo!
    Let's take a bath!
    🎭 Pretend to scrub arms and tummy: 搓搓搓 cuōcuōcuō.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 浴缸在哪裡？
    Yùgāng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the bathtub? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 浴缸裡面有什麼？
    Yùgāng lǐmiàn yǒu shénme?
    What's in the bathtub? (water, 水 shuǐ)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
