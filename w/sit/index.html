🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來坐下！
    Wǒmen lái zuòxià!
    Let's sit down!
    🎭 Stand up, then sit down together: plop!
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，我會坐下！
    Nǐ kàn, wǒ huì zuòxià!
    Look, I can sit down!
    🎭 Do it slowly and big.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    坐下！
    Zuòxià!
    Sit down!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any try counts.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    蜜蜜也會坐下！
    Mìmì yě huì zuòxià!
    Honey can sit down too!
    🎭 Point to the friend in the picture.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    好棒！
    Hǎo bàng!
    Great job!
    🎭 High five!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 蜜蜜在做什麼？
    Mìmì zài zuò shénme?
    What is Honey doing? (坐 zuò)
  2. 你會坐下嗎？
    Nǐ huì zuòxià ma?
    Can you sit down? Show me!
  3. 我們坐在哪裡？
    Wǒmen zuò zài nǎlǐ?
    Where do we sit? (on a chair, 椅子)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
