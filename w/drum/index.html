玩具 wánjù · Toys

鼓

gǔ

drum

← 氣球拼圖 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    鼓在哪裡？我們來找鼓！
    Gǔ zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo gǔ!
    Where is the drum? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then go and find the real toy.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是鼓。
    Duì! Zhè shì gǔ.
    Yes! This is the drum.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    打鼓，咚咚咚！
    Dǎ gǔ, dōngdōngdōng!
    Play the drum: boom boom boom!
    🎭 Drum on a pot with your hands: soft, then loud.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 鼓在哪裡？
    Gǔ zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the drum? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 鼓的聲音大還是小？
    Gǔ de shēngyīn dà háishì xiǎo?
    Is the drum loud or quiet?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
