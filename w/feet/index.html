身體 shēntǐ · Body

腳

jiǎo

feet

← 膝蓋

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    腳在哪裡？我們來找腳！
    Jiǎo zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo jiǎo!
    Where are your feet? Let's find them!
    🎭 Touch it on your own body first, then find it on the picture.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是腳。
    Duì! Zhè shì jiǎo.
    Yes! These are your feet.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    小腳踏踏踏！
    Xiǎo jiǎo tàtàtà!
    Little feet: stomp stomp stomp!
    🎭 March in place, then tiptoe softly.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 腳在哪裡？
    Jiǎo zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are your feet? (point to them)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 襪子穿在哪裡？
    Wàzi chuān zài nǎlǐ?
    Where do socks go? (on your feet)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
