家人 jiārén · Family

姐姐

jiějie

big sister

← 哥哥弟弟 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    姐姐在哪裡？我們來找姐姐！
    Jiějie zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo jiějie!
    Where is big sister? Let's go and look!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then to the real person if they are nearby (or a photo at home).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是姐姐。
    Duì! Zhè shì jiějie.
    Yes! This is big sister.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    姐姐在唱歌，啦啦啦！
    Jiějie zài chànggē, lālālā!
    Big sister is singing: la la la!
    🎭 Sing la-la-la together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 姐姐在哪裡？
    Jiějie zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is big sister? (point!)
  2. 這是誰？
    Zhè shì shéi?
    Who is this?
  3. 姐姐的頭髮長長的嗎？
    Jiějie de tóufǎ chángcháng de ma?
    Is big sister's hair long?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
