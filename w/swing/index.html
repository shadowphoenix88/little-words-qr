玩具 wánjù · Toys

鞦韆

qiūqiān

swing

← 溜滑梯

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    鞦韆在哪裡？我們來找鞦韆！
    Qiūqiān zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo qiūqiān!
    Where is the swing? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then go and find the real toy.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是鞦韆。
    Duì! Zhè shì qiūqiān.
    Yes! This is the swing.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    盪鞦韆，盪高高！
    Dàng qiūqiān, dàng gāogāo!
    On the swing: swing up high!
    🎭 Sway side to side together like a swing.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 鞦韆在哪裡？
    Qiūqiān zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the swing? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 你喜歡盪鞦韆嗎？
    Nǐ xǐhuān dàng qiūqiān ma?
    Do you like the swing?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
