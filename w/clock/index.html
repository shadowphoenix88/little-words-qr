時鐘

shízhōng

clock

鏡子 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    時鐘在哪裡？我們去找時鐘！
    Shízhōng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo shízhōng!
    Where is the clock? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是時鐘。
    Duì! Zhè shì shízhōng.
    Yes! This is the clock.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    滴答滴答！
    Dīdā dīdā!
    Tick tock, tick tock!
    🎭 Swing your arm like a pendulum.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 時鐘在哪裡？
    Shízhōng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the clock? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 時鐘說什麼？
    Shízhōng shuō shénme?
    What does the clock say? (滴答 dīdā)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
