身體 shēntǐ · Body

耳朵

ěrduo

ears

← 眼睛鼻子 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    耳朵在哪裡？我們來找耳朵！
    Ěrduo zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo ěrduo!
    Where are your ears? Let's find them!
    🎭 Touch it on your own body first, then find it on the picture.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是耳朵。
    Duì! Zhè shì ěrduo.
    Yes! These are your ears.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    聽聽看，什麼聲音？
    Tīngtīng kàn, shénme shēngyīn?
    Listen... what's that sound?
    🎭 Cup your hands behind your ears and listen together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 耳朵在哪裡？
    Ěrduo zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are your ears? (point to them)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 誰的耳朵長長的？
    Shéi de ěrduo chángcháng de?
    Whose ears are long? (the bunny's)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
