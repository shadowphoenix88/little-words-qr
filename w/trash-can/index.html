垃圾桶

lèsètǒng

trash can

← 鞋子

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    垃圾桶在哪裡？我們去找垃圾桶！
    Lèsètǒng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo lèsètǒng!
    Where is the trash can? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是垃圾桶。
    Duì! Zhè shì lèsètǒng.
    Yes! This is the trash can.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    丟進垃圾桶，耶！
    Diū jìn lèsètǒng, yē!
    Into the trash can, yay!
    🎭 Let your little one drop a clean tissue in.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 垃圾桶在哪裡？
    Lèsètǒng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the trash can? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 垃圾要丟到哪裡？
    Lèsè yào diū dào nǎlǐ?
    Where does trash go?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
