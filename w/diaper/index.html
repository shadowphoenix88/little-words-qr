尿布

niàobù

diaper

← 裙子睡衣 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    尿布在哪裡？我們來找尿布！
    Niàobù zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo niàobù!
    Where is your diaper? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then touch the real one (on you or in the drawer).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是尿布。
    Duì! Zhè shì niàobù.
    Yes! This is your diaper.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    換尿布，乾乾淨淨！
    Huàn niàobù, gāngān jìngjìng!
    New diaper, all clean and dry!
    🎭 Lie down, wiggle, then a big smile.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 尿布在哪裡？
    Niàobù zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is your diaper? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 尿布濕了嗎？
    Niàobù shī le ma?
    Is the diaper wet?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
