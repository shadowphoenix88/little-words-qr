玩具 wánjù · Toys

蠟筆

làbǐ

crayons

← 書氣球 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    蠟筆在哪裡？我們來找蠟筆！
    Làbǐ zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo làbǐ!
    Where are the crayons? Let's find them!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then go and find the real toy.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是蠟筆。
    Duì! Zhè shì làbǐ.
    Yes! These are the crayons.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    拿蠟筆，畫圈圈！
    Ná làbǐ, huà quānquān!
    Take a crayon and draw circles!
    🎭 Draw big circles together on paper.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 蠟筆在哪裡？
    Làbǐ zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are the crayons? (point to them)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 你要什麼顏色的蠟筆？
    Nǐ yào shénme yánsè de làbǐ?
    Which color crayon do you want?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
