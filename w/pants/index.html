褲子

kùzi

pants

← 衣服襪子 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    褲子在哪裡？我們來找褲子！
    Kùzi zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo kùzi!
    Where are your pants? Let's find them!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then touch the real one (on you or in the drawer).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是褲子。
    Duì! Zhè shì kùzi.
    Yes! These are your pants.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    穿褲子，腳伸進去！
    Chuān kùzi, jiǎo shēn jìnqù!
    Pants on: push your feet in!
    🎭 Let your little one push each leg through.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 褲子在哪裡？
    Kùzi zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are your pants? (point to them)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 褲子穿在哪裡？
    Kùzi chuān zài nǎlǐ?
    Where do pants go? (on your legs)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
