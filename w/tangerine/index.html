橘子

júzi

tangerine

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    橘子在哪裡？我們來找橘子！
    Júzi zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo júzi!
    Where is the tangerine? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then hold a real one if you have it.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是橘子。
    Duì! Zhè shì júzi.
    Yes! This is the tangerine.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    橘子剝開，香香的！
    Júzi bāokāi, xiāngxiāng de!
    Open the tangerine: smells so good!
    🎭 Peel and sniff; share the pieces.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 橘子在哪裡？
    Júzi zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the tangerine? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼水果？
    Zhè shì shénme shuǐguǒ?
    What fruit is this?
  3. 橘子是什麼顏色？
    Júzi shì shénme yánsè?
    What color is the tangerine?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
