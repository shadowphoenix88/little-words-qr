帽子

màozi

hat

← 襪子外套 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    帽子在哪裡？我們來找帽子！
    Màozi zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo màozi!
    Where is your hat? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then touch the real one (on you or in the drawer).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是帽子。
    Duì! Zhè shì màozi.
    Yes! This is your hat.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    戴帽子，出去玩！
    Dài màozi, chūqù wán!
    Hat on, let's go play outside!
    🎭 Put a hat on each other's heads.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 帽子在哪裡？
    Màozi zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is your hat? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 帽子戴在哪裡？
    Màozi dài zài nǎlǐ?
    Where does the hat go?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
