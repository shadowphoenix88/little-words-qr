水果 shuǐguǒ · Fruits

水梨

shuǐlí

pear

← 奇異果

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    水梨在哪裡？我們來找水梨！
    Shuǐlí zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo shuǐlí!
    Where is the pear? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then hold a real one if you have it.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是水梨。
    Duì! Zhè shì shuǐlí.
    Yes! This is the pear.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    水梨多汁，好甜！
    Shuǐlí duō zhī, hǎo tián!
    The pear is juicy and sweet!
    🎭 Pretend to bite and wipe your chin: so juicy!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 水梨在哪裡？
    Shuǐlí zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the pear? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼水果？
    Zhè shì shénme shuǐguǒ?
    What fruit is this?
  3. 水梨和蘋果，你喜歡哪個？
    Shuǐlí hé píngguǒ, nǐ xǐhuān nǎge?
    Pear or apple: which do you like?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
