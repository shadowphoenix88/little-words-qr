交通工具 jiāotōng gōngjù · Transportation

救護車

jiùhùchē

ambulance

← 消防車垃圾車 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    救護車在哪裡？我們來找救護車！
    Jiùhùchē zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo jiùhùchē!
    Where is the ambulance? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look for a real one outside.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是救護車。
    Duì! Zhè shì jiùhùchē.
    Yes! This is the ambulance.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    救護車要讓路！
    Jiùhùchē yào ràng lù!
    Make way for the ambulance!
    🎭 Step aside to the edge of the 'road' together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 救護車在哪裡？
    Jiùhùchē zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the ambulance? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 救護車要去幫助誰？
    Jiùhùchē yào qù bāngzhù shéi?
    Who is the ambulance going to help?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
