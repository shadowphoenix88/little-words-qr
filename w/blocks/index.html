玩具 wánjù · Toys

積木

jīmù

blocks

← 球娃娃 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    積木在哪裡？我們來找積木！
    Jīmù zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo jīmù!
    Where are the blocks? Let's find them!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then go and find the real toy.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是積木。
    Duì! Zhè shì jīmù.
    Yes! These are the blocks.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    積木疊高高，倒了！
    Jīmù dié gāogāo, dǎo le!
    Stack the blocks up high... crash!
    🎭 Build a tower, then knock it down together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 積木在哪裡？
    Jīmù zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are the blocks? (point to them)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 我們疊幾個積木？
    Wǒmen dié jǐ ge jīmù?
    How many blocks shall we stack?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
