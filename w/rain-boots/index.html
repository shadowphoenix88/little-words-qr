雨鞋

yǔxié

rain boots

← 雨衣

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    雨鞋在哪裡？我們來找雨鞋！
    Yǔxié zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo yǔxié!
    Where are your rain boots? Let's find them!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then touch the real one (on you or in the drawer).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是雨鞋。
    Duì! Zhè shì yǔxié.
    Yes! These are your rain boots.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    穿雨鞋，踩水坑！
    Chuān yǔxié, cǎi shuǐkēng!
    Rain boots on: splash in puddles!
    🎭 Stomp stomp like splashing in puddles.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 雨鞋在哪裡？
    Yǔxié zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are your rain boots? (point to them)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 雨鞋是什麼顏色？
    Yǔxié shì shénme yánsè?
    What color are the rain boots?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
