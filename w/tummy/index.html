身體 shēntǐ · Body

肚子

dùzi

tummy

← 手指膝蓋 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    肚子在哪裡？我們來找肚子！
    Dùzi zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo dùzi!
    Where is your tummy? Let's find it!
    🎭 Touch it on your own body first, then find it on the picture.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是肚子。
    Duì! Zhè shì dùzi.
    Yes! This is your tummy.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    肚子餓了，咕嚕咕嚕！
    Dùzi è le, gūlū gūlū!
    Tummy's hungry: rumble rumble!
    🎭 Pat your tummies and make rumbly sounds.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 肚子在哪裡？
    Dùzi zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is your tummy? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 你的肚子飽了嗎？
    Nǐ de dùzi bǎo le ma?
    Is your tummy full?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
