動物 dòngwù · Animals

大象

dàxiàng

elephant

← 馬猴子 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，大象來了！
    Nǐ kàn, dàxiàng lái le!
    Look, here comes the elephant!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then walk two fingers across the page.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    大象說：嗚！
    Dàxiàng shuō: wū!
    The elephant says toot!
    🎭 Use a big, silly voice.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    嗚！
    Wū!
    Toot!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any sound counts; cheer!
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來甩鼻子！
    Wǒmen lái shuǎi bízi!
    Let's swing our trunks!
    🎭 Arm in front of your nose like a trunk; swing it.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    大象，掰掰！
    Dàxiàng, bāibāi!
    Bye-bye, elephant!
    🎭 Wave goodbye to the picture.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 大象在哪裡？
    Dàxiàng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the elephant? (point to it)
  2. 大象怎麼叫？
    Dàxiàng zěnme jiào?
    What does the elephant say?
  3. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this? (any word or sound is a great answer)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
