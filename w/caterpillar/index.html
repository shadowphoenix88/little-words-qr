毛毛蟲

máomaochóng

caterpillar

← 蜻蜓蝸牛 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    毛毛蟲在哪裡？我們來找毛毛蟲！
    Máomaochóng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo máomaochóng!
    Where is the caterpillar? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page. Real bugs: look gently, no touching.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是毛毛蟲。
    Duì! Zhè shì máomaochóng.
    Yes! This is the caterpillar.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    毛毛蟲爬爬爬！
    Máomaochóng pápápá!
    The caterpillar crawls: wiggle wiggle!
    🎭 Lie on your tummy and wiggle forward.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 毛毛蟲在哪裡？
    Máomaochóng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the caterpillar? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼蟲？
    Zhè shì shénme chóng?
    What bug is this?
  3. 毛毛蟲長大會變成什麼？
    Máomaochóng zhǎng dà huì biànchéng shénme?
    What does a caterpillar turn into? (a butterfly)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
