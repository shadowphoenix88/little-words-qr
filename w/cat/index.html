動物 dòngwù · Animals

貓

māo

cat

← 狗兔子 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，貓來了！
    Nǐ kàn, māo lái le!
    Look, here comes the cat!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then walk two fingers across the page.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    貓說：喵喵！
    Māo shuō: miāomiāo!
    The cat says meow meow!
    🎭 Use a big, silly voice.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    喵喵！
    Miāomiāo!
    Meow meow!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any sound counts; cheer!
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來伸懶腰！
    Wǒmen lái shēn lǎnyāo!
    Let's stretch like a cat!
    🎭 Hands on the floor, bottom up, big stretch.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    貓，掰掰！
    Māo, bāibāi!
    Bye-bye, cat!
    🎭 Wave goodbye to the picture.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 貓在哪裡？
    Māo zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the cat? (point to it)
  2. 貓怎麼叫？
    Māo zěnme jiào?
    What does the cat say?
  3. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this? (any word or sound is a great answer)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
