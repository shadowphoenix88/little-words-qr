芭樂

bālè

guava

← 草莓鳳梨 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    芭樂在哪裡？我們來找芭樂！
    Bālè zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo bālè!
    Where is the guava? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then hold a real one if you have it.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是芭樂。
    Duì! Zhè shì bālè.
    Yes! This is the guava.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    芭樂脆脆的，好好吃！
    Bālè cuìcuì de, hǎo hǎochī!
    Guava is crunchy and yummy!
    🎭 Pretend to crunch a big bite.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 芭樂在哪裡？
    Bālè zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the guava? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼水果？
    Zhè shì shénme shuǐguǒ?
    What fruit is this?
  3. 芭樂是什麼顏色？
    Bālè shì shénme yánsè?
    What color is the guava?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
