窗戶

chuānghu

window

← 門床 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    窗戶在哪裡？我們去找窗戶！
    Chuānghu zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo chuānghu!
    Where is the window? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是窗戶。
    Duì! Zhè shì chuānghu.
    Yes! This is the window.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    窗戶外面有什麼？
    Chuānghu wàimiàn yǒu shénme?
    What's outside the window?
    🎭 Look out together and name what you see: 樹 tree, 車 car.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 窗戶在哪裡？
    Chuānghu zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the window? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 我們跟窗戶說早安！
    Wǒmen gēn chuānghu shuō zǎoān!
    Let's say good morning to the window!

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
