動物 dòngwù · Animals

猴子

hóuzi

monkey

← 大象

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，猴子來了！
    Nǐ kàn, hóuzi lái le!
    Look, here comes the monkey!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then walk two fingers across the page.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    猴子說：吱吱！
    Hóuzi shuō: zhīzhī!
    The monkey says ooh ooh!
    🎭 Use a big, silly voice.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    吱吱！
    Zhīzhī!
    Ooh ooh!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any sound counts; cheer!
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來抓抓頭！
    Wǒmen lái zhuāzhuā tóu!
    Let's scratch our heads!
    🎭 Scratch your head and tummy like a monkey.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    猴子，掰掰！
    Hóuzi, bāibāi!
    Bye-bye, monkey!
    🎭 Wave goodbye to the picture.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 猴子在哪裡？
    Hóuzi zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the monkey? (point to it)
  2. 猴子怎麼叫？
    Hóuzi zěnme jiào?
    What does the monkey say?
  3. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this? (any word or sound is a great answer)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
