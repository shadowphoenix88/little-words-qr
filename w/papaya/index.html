水果 shuǐguǒ · Fruits

木瓜

mùguā

papaya

← 芒果奇異果 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    木瓜在哪裡？我們來找木瓜！
    Mùguā zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo mùguā!
    Where is the papaya? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then hold a real one if you have it.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是木瓜。
    Duì! Zhè shì mùguā.
    Yes! This is the papaya.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    木瓜切開，裡面有黑黑的籽！
    Mùguā qiēkāi, lǐmiàn yǒu hēihēi de zǐ!
    Cut the papaya: little black seeds inside!
    🎭 Pretend to scoop out the seeds with a spoon.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 木瓜在哪裡？
    Mùguā zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the papaya? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼水果？
    Zhè shì shénme shuǐguǒ?
    What fruit is this?
  3. 木瓜裡面是什麼顏色？
    Mùguā lǐmiàn shì shénme yánsè?
    What color is the papaya inside?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
