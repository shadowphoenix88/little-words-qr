🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    芒果在哪裡？我們來找芒果！
    Mángguǒ zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo mángguǒ!
    Where is the mango? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then hold a real one if you have it.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是芒果。
    Duì! Zhè shì mángguǒ.
    Yes! This is the mango.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    芒果黃黃的，好香！
    Mángguǒ huánghuáng de, hǎo xiāng!
    The mango is yellow and smells good!
    🎭 Sniff a pretend mango: mmm!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 芒果在哪裡？
    Mángguǒ zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the mango? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼水果？
    Zhè shì shénme shuǐguǒ?
    What fruit is this?
  3. 夏天吃芒果，好嗎？
    Xiàtiān chī mángguǒ, hǎo ma?
    Shall we eat mango in summer?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
