玩具 wánjù · Toys

氣球

qìqiú

balloon

← 蠟筆鼓 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    氣球在哪裡？我們來找氣球！
    Qìqiú zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo qìqiú!
    Where is the balloon? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then go and find the real toy.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是氣球。
    Duì! Zhè shì qìqiú.
    Yes! This is the balloon.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    氣球飛高高！
    Qìqiú fēi gāogāo!
    The balloon floats up high!
    🎭 Tap a balloon up together. Popped balloon pieces are a choking risk: throw them away at once.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 氣球在哪裡？
    Qìqiú zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the balloon? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 氣球是什麼顏色？
    Qìqiú shì shénme yánsè?
    What color is the balloon?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
