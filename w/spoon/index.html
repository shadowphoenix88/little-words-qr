湯匙

tāngchí

spoon

← 碗牙刷 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    湯匙在哪裡？我們去找湯匙！
    Tāngchí zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo tāngchí!
    Where is the spoon? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是湯匙。
    Duì! Zhè shì tāngchí.
    Yes! This is the spoon.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    張開嘴巴！
    Zhāngkāi zuǐba!
    Open your mouth!
    🎭 Feed a stuffed animal with the spoon first.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 湯匙在哪裡？
    Tāngchí zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the spoon? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 我們用湯匙吃什麼？
    Wǒmen yòng tāngchí chī shénme?
    What do we eat with a spoon?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
