揮手

huīshǒu

wave

← 拍手抱抱 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來揮手！
    Wǒmen lái huīshǒu!
    Let's wave!
    🎭 Wave hello, then wave bye-bye.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，我會揮手！
    Nǐ kàn, wǒ huì huīshǒu!
    Look, I can wave!
    🎭 Do it slowly and big.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    揮手！
    Huīshǒu!
    Wave!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any try counts.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    琪琪也會揮手！
    Qíqí yě huì huīshǒu!
    Kiki can wave too!
    🎭 Point to the friend in the picture.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    好棒！
    Hǎo bàng!
    Great job!
    🎭 High five!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 琪琪在做什麼？
    Qíqí zài zuò shénme?
    What is Kiki doing? (揮手 huīshǒu)
  2. 你會揮手嗎？
    Nǐ huì huīshǒu ma?
    Can you wave? Show me!
  3. 我們什麼時候揮手？
    Wǒmen shénme shíhòu huīshǒu?
    When do we wave? (hello and bye-bye)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
