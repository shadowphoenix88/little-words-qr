交通工具 jiāotōng gōngjù · Transportation

捷運

jiéyùn

MRT (metro)

← 計程車火車 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    捷運在哪裡？我們來找捷運！
    Jiéyùn zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo jiéyùn!
    Where is the MRT train? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look for a real one outside.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是捷運。
    Duì! Zhè shì jiéyùn.
    Yes! This is the MRT train.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    坐捷運，抓好扶手！
    Zuò jiéyùn, zhuā hǎo fúshǒu!
    On the MRT: hold the handrail!
    🎭 Hold up a hand like holding a strap; sway.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 捷運在哪裡？
    Jiéyùn zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the MRT train? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 捷運門開了！我們上車嗎？
    Jiéyùn mén kāi le! Wǒmen shàng chē ma?
    The MRT doors opened! Shall we get on?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
