顏色形狀 yánsè xíngzhuàng · Colors & Shapes

橘色

júsè

orange

← 紅色黃色 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    橘色在哪裡？我們來找橘色！
    Júsè zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo júsè!
    Where is orange? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it, then find something in the room with that color or shape.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是橘色。
    Duì! Zhè shì júsè.
    Yes! This is orange.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    橘色的橘子！
    Júsè de júzi!
    An orange tangerine!
    🎭 Find something orange together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 橘色在哪裡？
    Júsè zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is orange? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 琪琪是什麼顏色？
    Qíqí shì shénme yánsè?
    What color is Kiki? (orange)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
