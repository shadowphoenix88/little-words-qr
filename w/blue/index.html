顏色形狀 yánsè xíngzhuàng · Colors & Shapes

藍色

lánsè

blue

← 綠色紫色 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    藍色在哪裡？我們來找藍色！
    Lánsè zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo lánsè!
    Where is blue? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it, then find something in the room with that color or shape.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是藍色。
    Duì! Zhè shì lánsè.
    Yes! This is blue.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    藍色的天空！
    Lánsè de tiānkōng!
    The blue sky!
    🎭 Find something blue together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 藍色在哪裡？
    Lánsè zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is blue? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 豆豆是什麼顏色？
    Dòudòu shì shénme yánsè?
    What color is Bean? (blue)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
