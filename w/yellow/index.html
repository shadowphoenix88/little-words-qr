顏色形狀 yánsè xíngzhuàng · Colors & Shapes

黃色

huángsè

yellow

← 橘色綠色 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    黃色在哪裡？我們來找黃色！
    Huángsè zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo huángsè!
    Where is yellow? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it, then find something in the room with that color or shape.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是黃色。
    Duì! Zhè shì huángsè.
    Yes! This is yellow.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    黃色的香蕉，彎彎的！
    Huángsè de xiāngjiāo, wānwān de!
    A yellow banana, curvy!
    🎭 Find something yellow together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 黃色在哪裡？
    Huángsè zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is yellow? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 蜜蜜是什麼顏色？
    Mìmì shì shénme yánsè?
    What color is Honey? (golden yellow)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
