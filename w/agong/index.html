家人 jiārén · Family

阿公

āgōng

Grandpa (阿公)

← 媽媽阿嬤 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    阿公在哪裡？我們來找阿公！
    Āgōng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo āgōng!
    Where is Grandpa? Let's go and look!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then to the real person if they are nearby (or a photo at home).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是阿公。
    Duì! Zhè shì āgōng.
    Yes! This is Grandpa.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    阿公好！
    Āgōng hǎo!
    Hello, Grandpa!
    🎭 Wave and bow a little: say hello.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 阿公在哪裡？
    Āgōng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is Grandpa? (point!)
  2. 這是誰？
    Zhè shì shéi?
    Who is this?
  3. 我們打電話給阿公，好嗎？
    Wǒmen dǎ diànhuà gěi āgōng, hǎo ma?
    Shall we call Grandpa?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
