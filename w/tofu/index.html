食物 shíwù · Food

豆腐

dòufu

tofu

← 水餃

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    豆腐在哪裡？我們來找豆腐！
    Dòufu zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo dòufu!
    Where is the tofu? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then at the table or in the kitchen.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是豆腐。
    Duì! Zhè shì dòufu.
    Yes! This is the tofu.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    豆腐白白的，軟軟的！
    Dòufu báibái de, ruǎnruǎn de!
    Tofu is white and soft!
    🎭 Wobble your hands like soft tofu.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 豆腐在哪裡？
    Dòufu zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the tofu? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 豆腐軟軟的還是硬硬的？
    Dòufu ruǎnruǎn de háishì yìngyìng de?
    Is tofu soft or hard?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
