動作 dòngzuò · Actions

睡覺

shuìjiào

sleep

← 喝走 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來睡覺！
    Wǒmen lái shuìjiào!
    Let's sleep!
    🎭 Hands under your cheek, eyes closed, little snore.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，我會睡覺！
    Nǐ kàn, wǒ huì shuìjiào!
    Look, I can sleep!
    🎭 Do it slowly and big.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    睡覺！
    Shuìjiào!
    Sleep!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any try counts.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    波比也會睡覺！
    Bōbǐ yě huì shuìjiào!
    Poppy can sleep too!
    🎭 Point to the friend in the picture.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    好棒！
    Hǎo bàng!
    Great job!
    🎭 High five!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 波比在做什麼？
    Bōbǐ zài zuò shénme?
    What is Poppy doing? (睡覺 shuìjiào)
  2. 你會睡覺嗎？
    Nǐ huì shuìjiào ma?
    Can you sleep? Show me!
  3. 我們在哪裡睡覺？
    Wǒmen zài nǎlǐ shuìjiào?
    Where do we sleep? (in bed, 床)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
