牙刷

yáshuā

toothbrush

← 湯匙鞋子 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    牙刷在哪裡？我們去找牙刷！
    Yáshuā zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo yáshuā!
    Where is the toothbrush? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是牙刷。
    Duì! Zhè shì yáshuā.
    Yes! This is the toothbrush.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    刷刷刷，刷牙！
    Shuāshuāshuā, shuāyá!
    Brush brush brush, brush your teeth!
    🎭 Brush a toy's teeth first, then your little one's.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 牙刷在哪裡？
    Yáshuā zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the toothbrush? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 你的牙齒在哪裡？
    Nǐ de yáchǐ zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are your teeth?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
