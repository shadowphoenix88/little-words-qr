食物 shíwù · Food

飯

fàn

rice

麵 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    飯在哪裡？我們來找飯！
    Fàn zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo fàn!
    Where is the rice? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then at the table or in the kitchen.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是飯。
    Duì! Zhè shì fàn.
    Yes! This is the rice.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    吃飯囉！好好吃！
    Chīfàn luo! Hǎo hǎochī!
    Time to eat! Yummy!
    🎭 Pretend to scoop rice and take a big bite.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 飯在哪裡？
    Fàn zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the rice? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 飯是白色的嗎？
    Fàn shì báisè de ma?
    Is rice white?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
