🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來跳高高！
    Wǒmen lái tiào gāogāo!
    Let's jump up high!
    🎭 Bend your knees and jump; hold hands.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，我會跳高高！
    Nǐ kàn, wǒ huì tiào gāogāo!
    Look, I can jump up high!
    🎭 Do it slowly and big.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    跳高高！
    Tiào gāogāo!
    Jump up high!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any try counts.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    波比也會跳高高！
    Bōbǐ yě huì tiào gāogāo!
    Poppy can jump up high too!
    🎭 Point to the friend in the picture.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    好棒！
    Hǎo bàng!
    Great job!
    🎭 High five!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 波比在做什麼？
    Bōbǐ zài zuò shénme?
    What is Poppy doing? (跳 tiào)
  2. 你會跳高高嗎？
    Nǐ huì tiào gāogāo ma?
    Can you jump up high? Show me!
  3. 你可以跳多高？
    Nǐ kěyǐ tiào duō gāo?
    How high can you jump?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
