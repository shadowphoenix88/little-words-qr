動作 dòngzuò · Actions

拍手

pāishǒu

clap

← 坐揮手 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來拍手！
    Wǒmen lái pāishǒu!
    Let's clap our hands!
    🎭 Clap fast, clap slow, clap high, clap low.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，我會拍手！
    Nǐ kàn, wǒ huì pāishǒu!
    Look, I can clap our hands!
    🎭 Do it slowly and big.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    拍手！
    Pāishǒu!
    Clap our hands!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any try counts.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    豆豆也會拍手！
    Dòudòu yě huì pāishǒu!
    Bean can clap our hands too!
    🎭 Point to the friend in the picture.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    好棒！
    Hǎo bàng!
    Great job!
    🎭 High five!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 豆豆在做什麼？
    Dòudòu zài zuò shénme?
    What is Bean doing? (拍手 pāishǒu)
  2. 你會拍手嗎？
    Nǐ huì pāishǒu ma?
    Can you clap our hands? Show me!
  3. 拍手會有什麼聲音？
    Pāishǒu huì yǒu shénme shēngyīn?
    What sound does clapping make?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
