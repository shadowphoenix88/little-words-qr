動物 dòngwù · Animals

馬

mǎ

horse

← 豬大象 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，馬來了！
    Nǐ kàn, mǎ lái le!
    Look, here comes the horse!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then walk two fingers across the page.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    馬說：噠噠噠！
    Mǎ shuō: dādādā!
    The horse says clip-clop!
    🎭 Use a big, silly voice.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    噠噠噠！
    Dādādā!
    Clip-clop!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any sound counts; cheer!
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來騎馬！
    Wǒmen lái qímǎ!
    Let's ride a horse!
    🎭 Little one sits on your knee; bounce gently.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    馬，掰掰！
    Mǎ, bāibāi!
    Bye-bye, horse!
    🎭 Wave goodbye to the picture.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 馬在哪裡？
    Mǎ zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the horse? (point to it)
  2. 馬怎麼叫？
    Mǎ zěnme jiào?
    What does the horse say?
  3. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this? (any word or sound is a great answer)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
