動作 dòngzuò · Actions

洗手

xǐshǒu

wash hands

← 抱抱唱歌 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來洗手！
    Wǒmen lái xǐshǒu!
    Let's wash our hands!
    🎭 Rub your palms together: scrub, scrub, rinse.
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，我會洗手！
    Nǐ kàn, wǒ huì xǐshǒu!
    Look, I can wash our hands!
    🎭 Do it slowly and big.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    洗手！
    Xǐshǒu!
    Wash our hands!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any try counts.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    豆豆也會洗手！
    Dòudòu yě huì xǐshǒu!
    Bean can wash our hands too!
    🎭 Point to the friend in the picture.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    好棒！
    Hǎo bàng!
    Great job!
    🎭 High five!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 豆豆在做什麼？
    Dòudòu zài zuò shénme?
    What is Bean doing? (洗手 xǐshǒu)
  2. 你會洗手嗎？
    Nǐ huì xǐshǒu ma?
    Can you wash our hands? Show me!
  3. 我們什麼時候洗手？
    Wǒmen shénme shíhòu xǐshǒu?
    When do we wash our hands? (before eating)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
