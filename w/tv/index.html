電視

diànshì

TV

← 樓梯

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    電視在哪裡？我們去找電視！
    Diànshì zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo diànshì!
    Where is the TV? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是電視。
    Duì! Zhè shì diànshì.
    Yes! This is the TV.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    電視關掉，我們來玩！
    Diànshì guāndiào, wǒmen lái wán!
    TV off, let's play!
    🎭 Point to the dark screen and find your reflections.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 電視在哪裡？
    Diànshì zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the TV? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 電視是黑色的嗎？
    Diànshì shì hēisè de ma?
    Is the TV black?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
