🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    爸爸在哪裡？我們來找爸爸！
    Bàba zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo bàba!
    Where is Daddy? Let's go and look!
    🎭 Point to the picture, then to the real person if they are nearby (or a photo at home).
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是爸爸。
    Duì! Zhè shì bàba.
    Yes! This is Daddy.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    爸爸抱抱，舉高高！
    Bàba bàobào, jǔ gāogāo!
    Daddy hug, up up high!
    🎭 Daddy lifts the little one up gently.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 爸爸在哪裡？
    Bàba zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is Daddy? (point!)
  2. 這是誰？
    Zhè shì shéi?
    Who is this?
  3. 爸爸在做什麼？
    Bàba zài zuò shénme?
    What is Daddy doing?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
