🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    我們來吃飯！
    Wǒmen lái chīfàn!
    Let's eat!
    🎭 Pretend to eat with a spoon: mmm!
  2. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    你看，我會吃飯！
    Nǐ kàn, wǒ huì chīfàn!
    Look, I can eat!
    🎭 Do it slowly and big.
  3. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    吃飯！
    Chīfàn!
    Eat!
    🎭 Little one's turn. Any try counts.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    蜜蜜也會吃飯！
    Mìmì yě huì chīfàn!
    Honey can eat too!
    🎭 Point to the friend in the picture.
  5. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    好棒！
    Hǎo bàng!
    Great job!
    🎭 High five!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 蜜蜜在做什麼？
    Mìmì zài zuò shénme?
    What is Honey doing? (吃 chī)
  2. 你會吃飯嗎？
    Nǐ huì chīfàn ma?
    Can you eat? Show me!
  3. 我們用什麼吃飯？
    Wǒmen yòng shénme chīfàn?
    What do we eat with? (a spoon, 湯匙)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
