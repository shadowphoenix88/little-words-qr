房子 fángzi · House

燈

dēng

light

← 冰箱馬桶 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    燈在哪裡？我們去找燈！
    Dēng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo dēng!
    Where is the light? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是燈。
    Duì! Zhè shì dēng.
    Yes! This is the light.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    開燈！關燈！
    Kāi dēng! Guān dēng!
    Lights on! Lights off!
    🎭 Flip the switch together (your hand on the switch too).

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 燈在哪裡？
    Dēng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the light? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 燈亮了嗎？
    Dēng liàng le ma?
    Is the light on?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
