交通工具 jiāotōng gōngjù · Transportation

火車

huǒchē

train

← 捷運飛機 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    火車在哪裡？我們來找火車！
    Huǒchē zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo huǒchē!
    Where is the train? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look for a real one outside.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是火車。
    Duì! Zhè shì huǒchē.
    Yes! This is the train.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    火車開動，嘟嘟！
    Huǒchē kāidòng, dūdū!
    The train is going: choo choo!
    🎭 Hold the waist of the person in front and chug around.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 火車在哪裡？
    Huǒchē zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the train? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 火車長長的還是短短的？
    Huǒchē chángcháng de háishì duǎnduǎn de?
    Is the train long or short?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
