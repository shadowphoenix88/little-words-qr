身體 shēntǐ · Body

嘴巴

zuǐba

mouth

← 鼻子牙齒 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    嘴巴在哪裡？我們來找嘴巴！
    Zuǐba zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo zuǐba!
    Where is your mouth? Let's find it!
    🎭 Touch it on your own body first, then find it on the picture.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是嘴巴。
    Duì! Zhè shì zuǐba.
    Yes! This is your mouth.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    嘴巴張開，啊！
    Zuǐba zhāngkāi, a!
    Open your mouth: aaah!
    🎭 Open wide like at the dentist, then close: chomp.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 嘴巴在哪裡？
    Zuǐba zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is your mouth? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 我們用嘴巴做什麼？
    Wǒmen yòng zuǐba zuò shénme?
    What do we do with our mouth? (eat, talk, sing)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
