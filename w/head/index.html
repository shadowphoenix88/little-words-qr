🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    頭在哪裡？我們來找頭！
    Tóu zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo tóu!
    Where is your head? Let's find it!
    🎭 Touch it on your own body first, then find it on the picture.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是頭。
    Duì! Zhè shì tóu.
    Yes! This is your head.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    摸摸頭，乖乖！
    Mōmō tóu, guāiguāi!
    Pat pat your head, good job!
    🎭 Gently pat each other's heads.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 頭在哪裡？
    Tóu zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is your head? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 帽子戴在哪裡？
    Màozi dài zài nǎlǐ?
    Where does a hat go? (on your head)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
