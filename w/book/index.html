玩具 wánjù · Toys

書

shū

book

← 小汽車蠟筆 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    書在哪裡？我們來找書！
    Shū zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo shū!
    Where is the book? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then go and find the real toy.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是書。
    Duì! Zhè shì shū.
    Yes! This is the book.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    打開書，我們來看！
    Dǎkāi shū, wǒmen lái kàn!
    Open the book, let's look!
    🎭 Let your little one turn the page.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 書在哪裡？
    Shū zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the book? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 你最喜歡哪本書？
    Nǐ zuì xǐhuān nǎ běn shū?
    Which book do you like best?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
