汽車

qìchē

car

機車 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    汽車在哪裡？我們來找汽車！
    Qìchē zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo qìchē!
    Where is the car? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look for a real one outside.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是汽車。
    Duì! Zhè shì qìchē.
    Yes! This is the car.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    開汽車，叭叭！
    Kāi qìchē, bābā!
    Drive the car: beep beep!
    🎭 Hold a pretend steering wheel and turn.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 汽車在哪裡？
    Qìchē zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the car? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 汽車有幾個輪子？
    Qìchē yǒu jǐ ge lúnzi?
    How many wheels does a car have?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
