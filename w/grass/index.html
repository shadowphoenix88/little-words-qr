自然 zìrán · Nature

草

cǎo

grass

← 花山 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    草在哪裡？我們來找草！
    Cǎo zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo cǎo!
    Where is the grass? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look out of the window for the real one.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是草。
    Duì! Zhè shì cǎo.
    Yes! This is the grass.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    草地上，光腳走走！
    Cǎodì shàng, guāng jiǎo zǒuzǒu!
    On the grass: walk with bare feet!
    🎭 Feel the grass (or a soft rug) with bare feet.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 草在哪裡？
    Cǎo zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the grass? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 誰喜歡吃草？
    Shéi xǐhuān chī cǎo?
    Who likes to eat grass? (the cow!)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
