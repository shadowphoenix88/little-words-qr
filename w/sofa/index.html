沙發

shāfā

sofa

← 椅子冰箱 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    沙發在哪裡？我們去找沙發！
    Shāfā zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo shāfā!
    Where is the sofa? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是沙發。
    Duì! Zhè shì shāfā.
    Yes! This is the sofa.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    沙發軟軟的！
    Shāfā ruǎnruǎn de!
    The sofa is soft and squishy!
    🎭 Pat the cushions and snuggle up.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 沙發在哪裡？
    Shāfā zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the sofa? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 誰坐在沙發上？
    Shéi zuò zài shāfā shàng?
    Who is sitting on the sofa?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
