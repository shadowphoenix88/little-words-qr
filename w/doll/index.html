玩具 wánjù · Toys

娃娃

wáwa

doll

← 積木小汽車 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    娃娃在哪裡？我們來找娃娃！
    Wáwa zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo wáwa!
    Where is the doll? Let's find it!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then go and find the real toy.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是娃娃。
    Duì! Zhè shì wáwa.
    Yes! This is the doll.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    娃娃睏了，拍拍她。
    Wáwa kùn le, pāipāi tā.
    Dolly is sleepy; pat pat her.
    🎭 Rock and pat the doll gently.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 娃娃在哪裡？
    Wáwa zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the doll? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 娃娃要吃飯嗎？
    Wáwa yào chīfàn ma?
    Does Dolly want to eat?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
