房子 fángzi · House

床

chuáng

bed

← 窗戶桌子 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    床在哪裡？我們去找床！
    Chuáng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo chuáng!
    Where is the bed? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是床。
    Duì! Zhè shì chuáng.
    Yes! This is the bed.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    躺下來，晚安！
    Tǎng xiàlái, wǎnān!
    Lie down... good night!
    🎭 Lie down, close your eyes, little snore, then pop up!

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 床在哪裡？
    Chuáng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the bed? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 我們在床上做什麼？
    Wǒmen zài chuáng shàng zuò shénme?
    What do we do in bed? (sleep, 睡覺)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
