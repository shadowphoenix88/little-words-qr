自然 zìrán · Nature

雨

yǔ

rain

← 雲彩虹 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    雨在哪裡？我們來找雨！
    Yǔ zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo yǔ!
    Where is the rain? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look out of the window for the real one.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是雨。
    Duì! Zhè shì yǔ.
    Yes! This is the rain.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    下雨了，滴滴答答！
    Xiàyǔ le, dīdī dādā!
    It's raining: drip drop drip drop!
    🎭 Tap your fingertips on the table like rain.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 雨在哪裡？
    Yǔ zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the rain? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 下雨要拿什麼？
    Xiàyǔ yào ná shénme?
    What do we take when it rains? (an umbrella, 雨傘)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
