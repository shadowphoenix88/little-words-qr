椅子

yǐzi

chair

← 桌子沙發 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    椅子在哪裡？我們去找椅子！
    Yǐzi zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen qù zhǎo yǐzi!
    Where is the chair? Let's go find it!
    🎭 Hold hands and walk around the room.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points to it or pats the sticker.
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是椅子。
    Duì! Zhè shì yǐzi.
    Yes! This is the chair.
    🎭 Touch it together and say the word slowly.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    請坐！
    Qǐng zuò!
    Please sit!
    🎭 Pull the chair out and sit together.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 椅子在哪裡？
    Yǐzi zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the chair? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 椅子是用來做什麼的？
    Yǐzi shì yònglái zuò shénme de?
    What is a chair for? (sitting, 坐)

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
