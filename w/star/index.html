自然 zìrán · Nature

星星

xīngxīng

star

← 月亮天空 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    星星在哪裡？我們來找星星！
    Xīngxīng zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo xīngxīng!
    Where is the star? Let's find it!
    🎭 Point to it on the page, then look out of the window for the real one.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是星星。
    Duì! Zhè shì xīngxīng.
    Yes! This is the star.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    一閃一閃亮晶晶！
    Yì shǎn yì shǎn liàngjīngjīng!
    Twinkle, twinkle, little star!
    🎭 Open and close your hands like twinkling stars.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 星星在哪裡？
    Xīngxīng zài nǎlǐ?
    Where is the star? (point to it)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 天上有幾顆星星？
    Tiānshàng yǒu jǐ kē xīngxīng?
    How many stars are in the sky?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
