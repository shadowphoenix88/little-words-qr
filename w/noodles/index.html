食物 shíwù · Food

麵

miàn

noodles

← 飯麵包 →

🎭 演一演 Role-play: act it out

  1. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    麵在哪裡？我們來找麵！
    Miàn zài nǎlǐ? Wǒmen lái zhǎo miàn!
    Where are the noodles? Let's find them!
    🎭 Find it on the page, then at the table or in the kitchen.
  2. 寶寶 bǎobǎo · Little one
    這裡！
    Zhèlǐ!
    Here!
    🎭 Little one points or pats the picture. Any point counts; cheer!
  3. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    對！這是麵。
    Duì! Zhè shì miàn.
    Yes! These are the noodles.
    🎭 Say the word slowly, then let your little one try.
  4. 大人 dàrén · Grown-up
    吸麵條，咻咻咻！
    Xī miàntiáo, xiūxiūxiū!
    Slurp the noodles: shloop!
    🎭 Pretend to slurp a long noodle.

❓ 問一問 Ask your little one

  1. 麵在哪裡？
    Miàn zài nǎlǐ?
    Where are the noodles? (point to them)
  2. 這是什麼？
    Zhè shì shénme?
    What is this?
  3. 麵條長長的還是短短的？
    Miàntiáo chángcháng de háishì duǎnduǎn de?
    Are noodles long or short?

Ask, wait 5 seconds, then answer it yourself with a smile. Pointing, sounds and words all count.
